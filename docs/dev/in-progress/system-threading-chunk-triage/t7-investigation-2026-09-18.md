## F1-continuewith

All 40 F1-continuewith failures are the `_0` variant of each ContinueWith overload (ATG's null-argument set). Three distinct defects stack: (1) the delegate-only overloads ARE correctly shape-wired to chaos_task_continue_with, but the ATG fixture passes `default(Action<Task>)!` and the native stub returns 0 on a null continuation instead of raising ArgumentNullException (which real .NET does — verified locally) → 20 items wired-wrong-semantic; (2) the CT/scheduler/options/state overloads are deliberately rejected by the shape resolver's arity!=1 guard and then fall into an operand-less 0-arg catch-all stub that discards receiver, delegate and options → 20 items orphan-catchall; (3) every case builds its receiver via SubjectInstanceFactory.Create<Task>() = GetUninitializedObject, and the emitted call is mis-instantiated as `...Create_System_Action__generic` — the antecedent is not a valid Task. Fixing (1)+(2) is the high-yield path (40/116 of the chunk); (3) needs ATG-side design. Crucially, no shape-registration signup is missing for the delegate-only case — the bug is the null-handling contract and the catch-all fallback, not the wiring.
